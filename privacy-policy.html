Privacy Policy — Bayt Al-Hikma

Last updated: [DATE]

This is a starting draft based on how Bayt Al-Hikma actually works today. It is not legal advice. A lawyer should review this before it's relied upon publicly, especially given real payments and personal data are involved across multiple countries.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains what information Bayt Al-Hikma ("we," "us," "the site") collects when you use the site, why we collect it, and what your rights are. By creating an account or using the site, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Your email address and password, when you create an account (your password is never stored in readable form — it's securely hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Your stated learning goals, level, format preference, language preference, and time-commitment preference, entered when building a reading/learning path.

Information generated as you use the site:

  • The reading paths you create (your goal, the books/videos/articles selected for you, and their sequence).
  • Your progress on each path (which items you've marked as read/watched).
  • Your subscription status (free or premium).

Information we do NOT collect:

  • We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. Payment processing is handled entirely by our payment provider (currently Stripe, or an alternative depending on your country), who has their own privacy policy governing that information.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To generate your personalized reading/learning paths.
  • To track and display your progress.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription (via our payment provider).
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g., password reset emails).

4. Third parties we share information with

To function, Bayt Al-Hikma sends certain information to the following third-party services:

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to keep you logged in between visits, via our authentication provider. This is necessary for the site to function and isn't used for advertising or tracking you across other websites.

6. International data transfers

Because we use services like Supabase, Anthropic, and Google, your information may be processed on servers located outside your own country. By using the site, you consent to this transfer.

7. Your rights

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, or request deletion of your account and associated data.

Currently, account deletion is handled manually — email us at [CONTACT EMAIL] to request it, since a self-service deletion option isn't built into the site yet.

8. Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data within a reasonable time, except where we're required to keep certain records (e.g., payment records) for legal or accounting reasons.

9. Children's privacy

Bayt Al-Hikma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information (e.g., encrypted password storage, HTTPS). However, no online service can guarantee absolute security, and we cannot promise your information will never be accessed by an unauthorized party.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we'll make a reasonable effort to notify you (e.g., a notice on the site).
